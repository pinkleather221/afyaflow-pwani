# Demo Script

## Golden scenario

A health worker reports that ORS sachets are almost out at a frontline facility. AfyaFlow Pwani extracts the report, confirms the fields, calculates a red stock-out warning, recommends a synthetic transfer option, and drafts a short handoff message.

## Pitch structure

1. Show the paper or voice input.
2. Show Gemma 4 extraction into structured fields.
3. Confirm the fields.
4. Show deterministic risk calculation.
5. Show redistribution recommendation.
6. Show audit trail and safety boundary.
