# Kaggle Write-up Draft

## Title

AfyaFlow Pwani: From Paper Stock Cards to Action in Seconds

## Problem

Primary and community health centres can miss early operational signals when stock reports, test-kit availability, and demand changes remain fragmented across paper cards and phone calls.

## Solution

AfyaFlow Pwani uses Gemma 4 to transform multilingual, unstructured facility input into verified inventory records, deterministic stock-out warnings, and explainable redistribution recommendations.

## Why Gemma 4

Gemma 4 is central to the workflow because it handles multilingual input, multimodal extraction, and constrained tool calling while supporting an offline-first deployment story.

## Safety

The prototype uses synthetic data only, requires human confirmation, avoids clinical advice, and keeps stock-risk arithmetic deterministic.
